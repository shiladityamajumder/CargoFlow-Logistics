Services at our Hamburg Location

From our Air & Sea location in Hamburg, we offer you comprehensive international transport and logistics solutions:

‍

  • Sea freight worldwide – FCL and LCL containers to all major overseas ports in Asia, North America, the Middle East, and beyond
  • International air freight – Import and export handling via Hamburg Airport and global hubs
  • Container rail connections to the Far East – Dedicated block trains Hamburg–China with 14–16 days transit time as a fast alternative to sea freight and a cost-efficient alternative to air freight
  • Port Handling & Container Trucking – Short distances to Hamburg's container terminals (Burchardkai, Eurogate, Tollerort) and container distribution to the hinterland

What international connections does Emons offer as a sea freight forwarder in Hamburg?

Via the Port of Hamburg, we organize weekly FCL and LCL transports to all major overseas ports in Asia, North America, the Middle East, Australia, and Africa. Our own locations in Hong Kong, Los Angeles, New York, Dubai, Chicago, and Detroit enable continuous support for your shipments from dispatch to delivery.

What does sea freight cost from Hamburg?

Sea freight costs depend on volume (m³), weight, destination port, season, and daily shipping company rates. Surcharges such as fuel (BAF) or security fees may also apply. Use our freight inquiry for a customized quote – free and non-binding. As a sea freight forwarder in Hamburg, we provide transparent conditions for FCL, LCL, and multimodal transport concepts.

‍

Where is the Emons Air & Sea location in Hamburg?

Our Hamburg branches are located in Hammerbrook (Spaldingstraße 74) and at the port in Veddel (Peutestraße 78). Both locations are excellently connected to the A1, A7, and A24 motorways and are in close proximity to the Burchardkai, Eurogate, and Tollerort container terminals, as well as Hamburg Airport.

‍

What are the advantages of the China–Hamburg container rail connection?

With Emons Rail Cargo, we operate our own block trains between Hamburg and the Far East. The transit time is 14 to 16 days – significantly faster than sea freight and at the same time considerably more cost-efficient than air freight. Multiple departures per week are available through the connection of various terminals to the rail network in China. An ideal multimodal solution for predictable supply chains.

‍

Does Emons also offer air freight and customs clearance in Hamburg?

Yes. In addition to our sea freight specialization, we offer comprehensive air freight services via Hamburg Airport at our Hamburg location, as well as complete customs clearance – including import/export customs declarations, T1 creation, and consultation by our own customs specialists. This way, you receive your international supply chain from a single source.
