Deliveries with truck-mounted forklift

flexible, safe and direct with Emons

When it comes to demanding deliveries, Emons offers the ideal answer for industry, trade and construction site logistics with its truck-mounted forklift solutions. Our specially equipped vehicles enable the reliable delivery of heavy or bulky goods, even in hard-to-reach locations — such as on construction sites, dead ends or on land without unloading equipment.

With a lifting capacity of up to 2,500 kg, all-wheel drive and a range of around 100 meters, our forklifts can handle rough terrain and tight access areas. This allows us to unload your goods directly at the point of use — across curbs, into driveways, carports or on hard-to-reach areas.
As an experienced trucking company, we guarantee maximum flexibility, punctuality and security — for every delivery that has special requirements. Whether on construction sites, when there are access restrictions or in inner-city areas: Emons ensures that your shipment arrives reliably.

  • on construction sites

  • beyond the curb on plots of land, in driveways, carports

  • in case of access restrictions for trucks

  • in dead ends without turning points

  • of large, long and bulky goods

  • of shipments that cannot be unloaded with a lift
