What is air freight?

Air freight refers to the shipment of goods by plane — the fastest and safest means of transportation.

How does air freight work?

An air freight forwarder books the freight space with the airline and organizes the entire shipping chain, including customs processing and onward transportation.

What does air freight cost?

Air freight is the most expensive delivery method, but it is also the fastest. The costs depend on weight, dimensions and destination.

What is transported by air freight?

Air freight is primarily used to transport time-sensitive, high-value, or fragile goods. These include spare parts, machine components, electronics, medicines, fashion items, and perishable goods such as food or flowers. Documents, express shipments, and urgent e-commerce orders are also frequently sent by air freight—anywhere where speed and reliability are crucial.

How does air freight have to be packaged?

The packaging must protect the goods during transport. Boxes, boxes or pallets are often used. Wood packaging must be treated in accordance with ISPM15 to prevent pests.
