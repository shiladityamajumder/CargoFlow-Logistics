Emons | DIGITAL

Emons | DIGITAL is the division of Emons that focuses on digital transformation, innovative business models and pioneering technologies in the logistics sector.

With our motto “Taking Tradition into the Future,” we have set ourselves the task of combining tradition and innovation.

Our goal is to use new potential and digital trends to make processes more efficient and to lead the logistics industry in the digital environment.

We not only want to react to developments in the context of digitization, but also actively shape them. This is what Emons | DIGITAL and, last but not least, Emons as an entire company stands for.

What is Emons Digital and what role does it play in Emons' digitization strategy?

With Emons Digital, we have created an area that focuses specifically on the strategic and operational development of digital topics at Emons.

We see digitization not only as a technological challenge, but as a holistic cultural change. Our digital strategy aims to make Emons future-proof — through new digital business models, agile processes and a lively culture of innovation.

Emons Digital works closely with other specialist departments to put digital solutions into practice and strengthen the future viability of our company.

How does Emons Digital support digitization within the company?

Emons Digital supports digitization by making innovation potential visible throughout the company. To this end, we are constantly talking to employees from different departments — from operations to administration.

Our goal is to create a realistic and common understanding of digital opportunities, to critically question existing processes and to develop new solutions together. In the long term, we are working to specifically increase Emons' digital maturity level — both technologically and culturally. Because digitization can only have a lasting effect if digital ways of thinking and working are anchored in everyday life.

What is our definition of digitization?

For us, digitization means not only the use of new technologies to increase efficiency, but also a fundamental economic and cultural change.
Emons Digital is accompanying this change strategically and methodically — with the aim of identifying and sustainably anchoring digital potential in the company.

We are not concerned with technical operations or infrastructure, but with new ways of thinking, business models and changes in cooperation.

Which companies does Emons Digital cooperate with?

We work with selected partners to implement digital innovations in a targeted and practical way. In close coordination with the specialist departments, we identify relevant use cases, evaluate suitable technologies and support the implementation in partnership — from prototype to scalable solution.

In doing so, we attach great importance to continuously dealing with current digital trends and technologies in order to identify new developments at an early stage and ensure that we are connected to digital progress. The aim is, together with internal and external partners, to create digital added value for Emons that have a long-term effect and contribute to the corporate strategy.
