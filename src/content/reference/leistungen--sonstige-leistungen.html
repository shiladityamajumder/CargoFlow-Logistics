Our network

As a founding member of the e.punkt disposal network, we offer a complete solution for the professional disposal of lithium-ion batteries — from packaging to transportation and recycling. Our Germany-wide presence makes it possible to provide suitable containers within 24 hours and to implement short transport routes, which saves time, emissions and costs. Rely on Emons | Battery for tailor-made solutions in the area of battery logistics and contribute to a sustainable battery cycle together with us.

Do you have any questions?
Find your answer here!

Does Emons guarantee compliance with all regulations?

Yes — Emons has all necessary approvals and has set up all processes in such a way that legally compliant and secure processing is guaranteed at all times.

Where does Emons offer its services?

Our services are available all over Europe — with consistently high quality and in compliance with all legal requirements.

How quickly can Emons pick up batteries or waste?

Usually within 5-7 working days. For recurring shipments, often faster — and on request, we offer express pickups within 24 hours. If you have special requirements, we will provide you with individual advice in advance.

What types of waste can Emons transport?

Our permits cover a wide range — we check each waste stream individually and implement tailor-made transport solutions.

Can Emons digitally sign transports via the eANV?

Yes Emons has its own access to the electronic waste certification process (eANV) and can therefore digitally apply for and sign disposal certificates.

Does Emons assist in lithium battery grading?

Absolutely. With the help of a detailed checklist, we help you to assess the condition of your batteries — as the basis for a tailor-made logistics solution.

What additional services does Emons offer?

In addition to battery logistics, we offer other services:

  • Logistics services via Emons Logistik
  • Air and sea freight via Emons Air & Sea
  • Rail transport with Emons Rail
  • Container pooling and transport packaging
  • Development and maintenance of waste notifications
  • Disposal solutions and return systems with partners
