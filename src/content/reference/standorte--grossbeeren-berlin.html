Freight Forwarding Berlin – Emons in Großbeeren (GVZ Berlin-Süd)

From the Berlin-Süd Freight Village in Großbeeren, located just a few kilometers south of the city limits, Emons organizes transport for the entire capital region. General cargo, part loads, and full loads are consolidated here using our own fleet and distributed via the national and European Emons network.

Berlin and Brandenburg are among the most dynamic logistics regions in Eastern Germany. Via the Berlin Ring (A10) and the B101, Emons reaches the districts of the capital as well as the surrounding area from Großbeeren – from Potsdam and Teltow to Ludwigsfelde – connecting regional distribution with supra-regional routes.

Services at this location

What the Großbeeren branch offers the capital region:

‍

  • General cargo for the capital region – Groupage freight is consolidated at the Großbeeren hub and transported further via fixed routes.
  • Part and full loads – Full and part load transport from Berlin-Brandenburg throughout Germany and across Europe.
  • Warehouse & distribution logistics – Order picking, temporary storage, and goods distribution for the Berlin-Brandenburg region.
  • Branch Delivery & E-Commerce – Recurring shipments, spare parts supply, and branch deliveries for retail and e-commerce.
  • Scheduled & Express Services – Time-critical orders via the Berlin Ring in all directions, wherever the route allows.
  • Combined Rail Transport – Linking truck and rail transport for suitable routes.
  • Dedicated On-Site Dispatch – Personal support in Großbeeren (managed by Christian Richter) instead of an anonymous hotline.

Where is the Emons location for Berlin?

In the Berlin-Süd freight village in Großbeeren (Märkische Allee 67), just a few kilometers south of the Berlin city limits, with access to the Berlin Ring (A10) via the B101.

These include Mitte, Charlottenburg-Wilmersdorf, Neukölln, Spandau, Tempelhof-Schöneberg, and Pankow, as well as the surrounding areas of Potsdam, Teltow, and Ludwigsfelde.

What makes the Berlin-Süd freight village location attractive for distribution?

The freight village allows shipments to be consolidated and efficiently distributed in all directions via the Berlin Ring, which reduces travel distances and transit times.
