Sustainable rail transport with Emons Rail Cargo

As early as 2006, Emons set an example for combined freight transport with its first own container train. Since 2010, we have been bundling our activities in the area of rail transport with the founding of Emons Rail Cargo GmbH. Objective: An efficient, environmentally friendly logistics solution for the future.

From our location in Dresden, we coordinate all rail traffic and develop innovative concepts for CO₂-reduced transport chains. The fact is: The transfer of goods from road to rail results in a significant reduction in exhaust gas and noise emissions per loading unit.

As a founding member of Kombiverkehr GmbH & Co. KG, we are actively committed to shifting transport to rail — in terms of sustainable logistics and environmental responsibility.

What is rail freight transport?

The transport of goods by rail using containers or swap bodies.

How does rail freight work?

Goods are packed into swap bodies or containers, loaded onto trains and transported to their destinations.

What does a freight train transport?

Freight trains transport units onto wagons. Such as containers, swap bodies, semi-trailers and special wagons. Most packaged goods from industry and trade are transported in swap bridges. Rail transport often involves moving bulk goods over long distances.
