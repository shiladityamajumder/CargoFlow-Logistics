An overview of our core services

Regardless of whether it's Europe, Asia or overseas — Emons brings your cargo to its destination all over the world. To ensure that your request is processed quickly and efficiently, simply contact the appropriate Emons branch. Our experts are at your side with individual solutions and coordinate your transport to the desired destination. You can find the office responsible for you via our location directory.

About our services

About our services

  • road traffic: Nationally & internationally by general cargo, part and full load

  • Air & sea freight: Direct shipments, consolidation & multimodal solutions

  • Rail logistics: Sustainable transport with connections to major economic centers

  • Express & special transport: Direct trips, CEP services & on-board courier

  • Dangerous goods logistics: Safe transport & storage of hazardous substances

  • Project & heavy transport: Individual solutions for oversized & heavy goods

  • Exhibition logistics: Planning, transport & handling for international trade fairs

  • Automotive & industrial logistics: Just-in-time & just-in-sequence solutions

  • warehouse logistics: Efficient storage solutions with flexible spaces

  • customs processing: Import & export customs services including advice

  • returns management: Efficient solutions for retail & e-commerce

How do I find an Emons branch near me?

Use the location overview to find an Emons branch near you. You can narrow down your selection using the search function and filters for country and region. On each location's detail page, you will find further information on services and contact options.

How many locations does Emons have?

Emons is represented worldwide with over 120 locations. In our location overview, you can filter branches by country and service area to access the specific details for each site.

What services does each location offer?

Services vary by location; not every branch offers the full range of transport and logistics solutions. You can see which services are available at a specific location in the overview and on the respective location detail page. For specific inquiries, please contact the relevant branch directly.

Who can I contact at my location?

You can find the contact details and representatives for each branch on its respective location page. Please use the phone numbers or email addresses provided there to discuss your transport request or any questions regarding local services.
