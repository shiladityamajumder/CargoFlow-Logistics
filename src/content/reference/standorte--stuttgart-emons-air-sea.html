Air & Sea Freight Stuttgart – Emons Air & Sea at the airport

Located directly in the air freight center at Stuttgart Airport, Emons Air & Sea manages your international air and sea freight shipments – from export processing to delivery at the final destination, with connections to overseas ports and cargo hubs around the globe.

The Stuttgart region is one of Europe's most export-oriented industrial hubs; automotive and high-tech companies ship goods from here to all corners of the world. Because the Stuttgart Trade Fair is located right next to the airport, the site is also a strong partner for trade fair and exhibition logistics – including customs clearance and project shipments.

Services at this location

What Emons Air & Sea provides at Stuttgart Airport:

  • Global air freight – Import and export services from Stuttgart Airport with connections to global cargo hubs.
  • FCL & LCL sea freight – Full and less-than-container load shipments to major overseas ports worldwide, organized through the international Emons network.
  • Trade fair & exhibition logistics – Located directly at the Stuttgart Trade Fair: booth deliveries, returns, and ATA Carnet processing for exhibitors.
  • Customs clearance – Import/export customs clearance, customs consulting, and special customs procedures, including temporary import/export via ATA Carnet.
  • Project & heavy-lift cargo – Tailored solutions for complex and oversized shipments.
  • Courier & special transport – In special cases, we also offer accompanied courier shipments.
  • Industry logistics – Production, distribution, and spare parts logistics for automotive and high-tech clients in the region.
  • Pre- and on-carriage for the air freight center – The STR air freight center is located outside the security zone and is accessible by truck from all sides.

Where is Emons Air & Sea located in Stuttgart?

In the air freight center directly at Stuttgart Airport (Luftfrachtzentrum 610/3, 70629 Stuttgart), on the Filder plateau and right next to the A8 motorway.

Does Emons also handle trade fair logistics at Stuttgart Airport?

Yes. Because the Stuttgart Trade Fair is located right next door, our team organizes booth deliveries, returns, and customs processing (ATA Carnets) for exhibitors.

Which destinations does Emons serve from Stuttgart via air and sea freight?

We connect to major overseas markets and global freight hubs via air freight and FCL/LCL sea freight; our team coordinates specific routes and ports for each individual shipment.

Does Emons also handle customs clearance in Stuttgart?

Yes, from import/export customs clearance and customs consulting to special customs procedures and temporary import/export via ATA Carnet.

How does this site differ from the Emons Ditzingen location?

This site covers air freight, sea freight, customs, and trade fair logistics. For general cargo, full-load transport, and warehouse logistics in the greater Stuttgart area, please contact our sister site in Ditzingen.
