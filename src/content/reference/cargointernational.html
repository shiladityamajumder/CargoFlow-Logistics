Frequently asked questions about shipping with Cargo International

How much does a freight forwarding service cost for private customers?

You can determine the price for your shipment using the Cargo International shipping calculator. You will also receive pricing for optional additional services there before you book your order online.

How do I book a freight carrier online?

Enter your pickup address, destination, and shipment details into the shipping calculator. Then, calculate the shipping costs, book your shipment, and pay online. Pickup is included in the order.

What shipments can I send as a private individual?

Cargo International offers shipping options for parcels, bulky goods, and palletized freight. Our services also include two-man delivery. The shipping method suitable for your shipment depends on the details of your goods.

How does the pickup process work?

You pack your shipment and provide the pickup address when booking online. Cargo International organizes the transport from the on-site pickup to delivery at the desired address. All shipping orders include pickup.

How long does a freight delivery take?

The duration of a freight delivery depends on the destination and the chosen shipping method. The specific delivery time is determined by the transport solution booked. You can track the progress of your shipment and arrival information using your tracking number.

What is the difference between parcel and freight shipping?

Parcel shipping is suitable for shipments that fall within the specifications of the respective parcel service. For larger, heavier goods or items packed on a pallet, freight shipping is the right choice. Cargo International offers various shipping methods to suit your specific shipment.
