Whistleblower system

In line with our corporate culture, our ethical principles and our company-wide compliance strategy, we offer a whistleblower system the opportunity to create notifications of violations of law, misconduct or unethical conduct.
We maintain open communication and investigate all indications of misconduct. If you would like to make a report regarding our company, please use the following link:

emons.whistleport.de

For data protection reasons (cookies) and to make it difficult to track the user, the link is not clickable. You must copy the URL into your browser's address bar. You can also submit the information completely anonymously. Your possible anonymity is ensured by several technical mechanisms.
